V-Board Privacy

V-Board stores whiteboards, lectures, annotations, generated vectors, and study history so signed-in users can access their work across devices.

Authentication uses Sign in with Apple. V-Board does not receive or manage an account password. Apple may provide a private relay email when a user chooses Hide My Email.

Photos, files, and Freeform-exported PDFs are uploaded only after the user explicitly imports them. When a user requests an AI study feature, the selected board content and the minimum relevant context may be sent to the configured AI model provider to produce that response.

Operational logs exclude authentication credentials, complete board images, and full prompts. Users can permanently delete their account and V-Board-owned data from Account settings.

Contact the V-Board operator for privacy questions or data requests.
